Linear Waste Model vs. ReNourish Circular Loop

The Traditional Landfill Trajectory

  • Surplus meals discarded in trash bins
  • Anaerobic decomposition in landfills generating toxic Methane (CH₄)
  • Complete loss of valuable soil nutrients and embodied water
  • Financial burden of municipal waste haulage & landfill fees

The Circular Recycling Ecosystem

  • Immediate surplus routing to human hunger relief shelters
  • Inedible peels & scraps upcycled into protein feed or bio-materials
  • Organic waste diverted to Anaerobic Digestion for clean Biogas & Energy
  • Nutrient-rich digestate returned to farm soil as humic fertilizer
